Osmium

A hexagonal core, bonded to four oxygen atoms, with a boot prompt inside. The mark combines three of the earlier directions: the crystal lattice, the tetroxide molecule and the terminal prompt. It is drawn entirely as shapes, with no lettering.

  • HEXAGONThe core is the cell of osmium’s close-packed lattice (No. 02).
  • FOUR BONDSOsO₄, osmium’s oxide. It works as a quiet nod to Rust (No. 06).
  • >_A prompt and cursor, where the OS hands control to you (No. 07).

Lockups

Use the horizontal lockup by default. The stacked lockup is for square spaces such as social avatars, stickers and title slides. The wordmark is set in JetBrains Mono SemiBold, in lowercase to match the crate name.

Color variants

Full color is the primary version. Use the reversed version on dark grounds. Use the one-color versions for single-ink printing, embossing and places where color isn’t available. In those, the hexagon becomes an outline so the bonds, prompt and atoms stay separate.

Small sizes

Below 40px the oxygen atoms turn to noise, so the mark switches to the simplified core: the hexagon with its prompt, with heavier strokes. Use the core for favicons, the docs.rs sidebar, app icons and CLI badges.

Clear space and construction

The unit is x, the diameter of one oxygen atom. Every measurement scales with the mark.

Clear space
Keep at least 1x of empty space on every side of the mark, measured from the outermost atoms.
Minimum size
Full mark: 40px or 10mm wide. Simplified core: 16px or 5mm.
Geometry
A pointy-top hexagon with a circumradius of 2.9x. The visible bonds run out from its centre to three atoms. A fourth, half-opacity atom sits behind it, up and to the right.
Don’t
Don’t rotate the mark, recolor the atoms individually, add a stroke around the hexagon, or swap the chevron for typed text.

Palette

Slate and lustre come from the metal itself. Oxide is the only warm color, and it is kept for the atoms and the cursor.

In use

A README header for GitHub and crates.io, and the favicon in a docs.rs browser tab.
